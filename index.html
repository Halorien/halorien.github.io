Mahjong Hand Renderer

Dora indicator
Compact:
(m = characters, p = circles, s = bamboo)
(0 = red five, z = honors, b = back, | = gap)
(1 = East, 2 = South, 3 = West, 4 = North, 5 = White, 6 = Green, 7 = Red)
(Calls: end a group with L/A/R to rotate the called tile, e.g. 111zL)
(Dora: enter the indicator, the renderer highlights the next tile: 9→1, 4z→1z, 7z→5z)
